Most common setup

  1. 1

    Check the model. This guide is for an HF680 whose part number includes -R.

  2. 2

    Choose the host interface. USB PC Keyboard is the factory default. Pick another interface only if your computer, till, or software needs it.

  3. 3

    Scan the chosen code. For Plug and Play interface codes, power-cycle the host afterwards.

Before you scan

These are programming commands, not product barcodes. Scan one setting at a time, keep the phone bright and steady, and avoid factory reset unless you intend to remove custom settings.

⌁Turn your iPhone brightness up, then hold the scanner about 15 to 25 cm from the code. Tap a code to make it full screen.
